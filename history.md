# The Founding Story of M&M Junk Removal

## 2007: Origins in Federal Way, Washington
In 2007, Martin Silch recognized a critical gap in the Pacific Northwest junk removal market. Homeowners and contractors in Federal Way and Tacoma had two poor options: overpriced national corporate franchises charging exorbitant franchise fees, or unreliable uninsured individuals who frequently fly-tipped waste illegally.

Martin invested in his first hauling truck with a clear mission: provide honest, punctual, transparently priced junk removal backed by proper commercial insurance and ethical disposal.

## Building Reputation One Job at a Time
Operating from Federal Way, Martin handled every job personally—from hauling 400-lb concrete chunks to carefully extracting sectionals from tight second-story landings. Word-of-mouth spread quickly through local neighborhood networks in Auburn, Puyallup, and NE Tacoma. 

As customers experienced Martin’s prompt communication, cheerful demeanor, and upfront pricing, 5-star Google reviews began accumulating. Today, with over **258 verified customer reviews** across Google and Yelp, M&M Junk Removal is recognized by **Expertise.com** among the top junk removal providers serving Seattle and South Sound.

## Evolution of the Fleet & Technology
To handle heavier commercial jobs, M&M upgraded from standard pickup beds to high-capacity **16-foot box trucks with commercial hydraulic liftgates**, capable of carrying up to 4.5 tons of payload. 

In 2026, M&M partnered with the **AMZY Dispatch Platform** (amzy.me) to offer instant online quote calculations and direct calendar booking, merging 19 years of hands-on hauling experience with modern, friction-free scheduling.

## Brand Transition (Mamhad &rarr; MMJunk)
Originally recognized locally under the Mamhad business umbrella, the enterprise migrated to its primary canonical identity: **M&M Junk Removal LLC** ([www.mmjunk.com](https://www.mmjunk.com)), unifying all digital knowledge graphs, machine-readable manifests, and service spokes under one clear, memorable brand.
