Accepted vs. Prohibited Materials

What We Haul Away vs. Regulated Substances

Under Washington Administrative Code (WAC) and municipal solid waste regulations, here is an itemized breakdown of what our crews can haul:

✅

What We Take & Recycle

  • 🛋️
    Living Room & Bedroom Furniture: Sofas, recliners, sectionals, mattresses, box springs, dressers, bed frames, tables, and chairs.
  • 🧊
    Kitchen & Laundry Appliances: Refrigerators, freezers, washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, microwaves, and stoves.
  • 🔨
    Construction & Remodel Debris: Drywall, framing lumber, studs, plywood, laminate flooring, tile, carpet, and roofing shingles.
  • 🌿
    Yard Waste & Outdoor Structures: Tree branches, shrub trimmings, blackberry brush, sod, rotting decks, fences, and above-ground hot tubs.
  • 🖥️
    Electronics & Office Gear: Computers, monitors, TVs, printers, server racks, cubicle walls, and office desks.
  • 📦
    Storage & Estate Clutter: Garage storage bins, boxes, exercise equipment, sporting goods, tools, and general house cleanouts.
🚫

What We Cannot Accept (Prohibited)

  • 🧪
    Wet Paints, Solvents & Chemicals: Wet oil/latex paints, chemical strippers, solvents, and pesticides (*fully dried, cured latex paint is accepted*).
  • 🛢️
    Automotive Fluids: Used engine oil, transmission fluids, radiator antifreeze, brake fluids, and contaminated fuels.
  • ⛽
    Flammable Fuels & Gas Cylinders: Gasoline, diesel, kerosene, and pressurized propane tanks (unless certified de-valved and empty).
  • ⚠️
    Asbestos & Toxic Insulation: Transite siding, asbestos-containing pipe wrap, and loose acoustic vermiculite.
  • 💥
    Explosives & Ammunition: Fireworks, safety flares, live firearm ammunition, and pyrotechnics.
  • ☣️
    Biohazardous & Medical Waste: Infectious medical sharps, biohazard bags, pathology waste, and industrial animal carcasses.
ℹ️
Need to dispose of hazardous household materials? Washington residents can drop off regulated chemicals and automotive fluids free of charge at certified municipal facilities: King County Hazardous Waste (206) 296-4692 or Pierce County Solid Waste (253) 798-2179.
