Heavy Concrete & Masonry Debris Removal: Tacoma, WA

On August 5, 2026, M&M Junk Removal completed the heavy extraction and transport of 3,850 pounds of solid broken concrete patio and foundation slabs from a residential property in Tacoma, WA. With individual slabs weighing up to 400 pounds each, our crew utilized 60-inch steel pry bars, all-terrain dollys, and reinforced ramps to load the material safely without damaging existing landscape or driveways, delivering 100% of the rubble directly to the Tacoma Recovery & Transfer Center for aggregate crushing.
