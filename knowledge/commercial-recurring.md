## Summary
M&M Junk Removal offers scheduled recurring junk removal routes and priority dispatch contracts for property managers, apartment complexes, commercial retail plazas, HOAs, and general contractors throughout South King and Pierce County, WA.

## Recurring Service Programs
1. **Apartment & HOA Dumpster Overflow Sweeps:** Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly sweep routes to clear bulky items dumped around corrals (mattresses, couches, broken appliances, TVs).
2. **Tenant Move-Out & Turnover Cleanouts:** Guaranteed 24-hour dispatch upon tenant eviction or vacancy to clear abandoned property and ensure fast turnover.
3. **Retail & Facility Backroom Debris Management:** Ongoing removal of broken pallets, broken fixtures, retail display cases, and packaging accumulation.
4. **General Contractor Live-Load Routes:** Scheduled pickup runs directly from active remodeling sites to prevent on-site roll-off dumpster permit hassles.
