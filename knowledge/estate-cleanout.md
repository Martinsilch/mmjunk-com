## Summary
M&M Junk Removal provides compassionate, professional, and time-sensitive estate cleanout services for executors, realtors, and families in Pierce and South King County, WA. We specialize in clearing properties efficiently while handling sensitive items with the care they deserve.

## The M&M Estate Process
1. **Initial Assessment & Walkthrough:** Free on-site visual inspection to determine the scope, volume, and timeline. Identification and preservation of family heirlooms, vital legal records, and sentimental items.
2. **Respectful Sorting Streams:** We categorize all items into four distinct streams:
   - **Family Keepsakes:** Items identified for family retention or secured transport.
   - **Donations:** Usable goods, furniture, clothing, and household items donated to local community charities (Goodwill, St. Vincent de Paul, local shelters) with donation receipts provided.
   - **Recycling:** E-waste, metals, appliances, and materials processed at certified transfer facilities (Bow Lake, Algona, Tacoma Recovery Center).
   - **Responsible Disposal:** Landfill diversion and legal disposal of remaining non-salvageable debris.
3. **Property Preparation:** We ensure the property is left "broom-clean" and market-ready for real estate staging, listing photography, or final transfer.
4. **Logistics & Coordination:** Seamless coordination with probate attorneys, real estate brokers, and out-of-state executors using secure lockbox entry and photographic verification.
