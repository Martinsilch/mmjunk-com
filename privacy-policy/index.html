Privacy Policy & Data Trust Architecture

Effective Date: October 2, 2026 • Governing M&M Junk Removal LLC • Canonical: mmjunk.com (Legacy: mamhad.com)

1. Operational Scope & Identity

M&M Junk Removal LLC (formerly operating as Mamhad Junk Removal) provides residential, commercial, and contractor junk hauling services across South King and Pierce Counties, Washington (including Federal Way, Auburn, Kent, Tacoma, Puyallup, and Edgewood).

This policy describes how we collect, process, and safeguard information submitted directly by customers or via our verified scheduling partner, the AMZY Dispatch Platform.

2. Strict No-Sale Commitment

We Never Sell Your Data

Your personal information, phone numbers, email addresses, and service addresses are never sold, rented, leased, or traded to third-party data brokers, marketers, or lead generation aggregators.

Data collected during estimate intake or appointment booking is used strictly for logistical coordination, route optimization, equipment dispatch, and verified transaction receipting.

3. Data Collection & Dispatch Intake

We collect information only when voluntarily submitted by you:

  • Contact Details: Name, phone number, and email address for dispatch notification and invoice delivery.
  • Service Location: Physical address and ZIP code to verify 15-mile operating radius and route scheduling.
  • Job Scope & Photos: Item descriptions, estimated volume, and item photos provided for accurate quote calculation.

4. Verified Partner Integration (AMZY Platform)

Online calendar reservations and instant quotes are facilitated through our verified dispatch software partner, AMZY (amzy.me).

AMZY operates under strict end-to-end transport encryption (TLS 1.3) and processes intake data strictly to schedule two-person truck crews and deliver automated SMS dispatch updates.
