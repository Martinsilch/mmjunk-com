# About M&M Junk Removal

> M&M Junk Removal is your premier, locally owned and operated, non-franchise junk removal and hauling specialist, proudly serving Federal Way, Auburn, Puyallup, and the broader Pierce County and South King County, WA regions since 2007.

## Our Story
M&M Junk Removal was founded in 2007 by **Martin Silch** to provide honest, dependable, and affordable hauling services to South Puget Sound communities. Frustrated by the excessive surcharges, corporate royalty markups, and impersonal call centers of national franchise haulers, Martin built M&M on a simple premise:
> *"Treat customers fairly, show up on time, provide upfront pricing, and haul junk responsibly."*

Over 19+ years of operation, M&M Junk Removal has completed thousands of successful residential cleanouts, commercial demolitions, and contractor hauls, earning over **258 verified customer reviews** (234 5.0-star Google reviews and 24 Yelp reviews) and an **AAA+ BBB Rating**.

## Non-Franchise Advantage: The 10% Rate Guarantee
Because M&M Junk Removal is independently owned with zero corporate franchise royalties, we maintain low operational overhead and pass those savings directly to local homeowners, realtors, and contractors. We back this with our written policy:
**We will match and beat any verified written estimate from a licensed Washington competitor by 10%.**

## Our Commitment to Landfill Diversion & the Community
We believe in responsible environmental stewardship. Every truckload collected is actively triaged into four streams:
1. **Charity Donation:** Furniture, clothing, tools, and household goods in usable condition are donated to local charities (Goodwill, St. Vincent de Paul, local shelters).
2. **Scrap Metal Recycling:** Structural steel, copper, appliances, and metals are delivered to certified scrap yards for industrial reprocessing.
3. **Clean Green Composting:** Yard waste, logs, brush, and timber are diverted to clean composting facilities.
4. **Certified E-Waste Recycling:** Electronics, computers, monitors, and components are recycled free of charge.
