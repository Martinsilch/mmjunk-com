# Estate Cleanout Protocols & Professional Services

M&M Junk Removal provides compassionate, thorough, and respectful whole-home cleanouts for estate executors, probate attorneys, surviving family members, and real estate professionals. Founded in 2007 by Martin Silch, our crews understand that clearing a loved one's property requires delicate handling, patience, and meticulous item categorization.

## 1. Multi-Stage Cleanout Process
1. **Initial Walkthrough & Item Preservation:** Before any removal, our team walks the property with the client or executor to identify and set aside heirlooms, legal documents, sentimental memorabilia, and items of monetary value.
2. **Eco-Conscious Sorting & Donation Delivery:** Usable furniture, household goods, clothing, and books are cataloged and transported to local Washington charity partners (Goodwill, St. Vincent de Paul, local shelters) for donation receipts.
3. **Scrap & Electronic Recycling:** Metals, wires, electronics, and broken appliances are diverted from landfills to authorized recycling transfer facilities (Bow Lake, Algona, Tacoma Recovery Center).
4. **Final Haul & Broom-Sweep:** Remaining debris is hauled away in high-capacity 16ft box trucks, leaving the home, garage, and yard broom-clean and market-ready.

## 2. Realtor & Property Manager Coordination
- **Fast Turnaround:** Available for 24-48 hour turnaround to meet strict listing deadlines or open house schedules.
- **Lockbox / Remote Access:** We frequently coordinate with out-of-state executors using secure lockbox entry, providing before-and-after photo documentation.
- **Commercial Billing:** Detailed invoicing provided for estate accounting, tax filing, and probate reimbursement.
