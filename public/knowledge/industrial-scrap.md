## Summary
M&M Junk Removal provides commercial and industrial cleanouts, heavy scrap metal extraction, machinery recycling, and warehouse turnover hauling across South King and Pierce County, WA. Equipped with 16-foot box trucks rated up to 4.5 tons payload with commercial hydraulic liftgates, we handle heavy materials that standard residential haulers reject.

## Industrial Scrap Capabilities
1. **Scrap Metal Recycling & Salvage:** Ferrous and non-ferrous metal recycling including structural steel, iron beams, copper piping, HVAC units, commercial refrigeration compressors, and automotive parts.
2. **Machinery & Equipment Extraction:** Safe rigging, disassembling, and hauling of obsolete factory machinery, industrial prep tables, commercial boilers, lathes, conveyor sections, and manufacturing tools.
3. **Warehouse & Logistics Turnovers:** Complete clearing of abandoned wooden pallets, shrink-wrap bales, industrial shelving, pallet racking, damaged freight, and packaging debris.
4. **Heavy Masonry & Demolition Scrap:** Slabs of broken concrete (up to 400+ lbs per chunk), cinderblocks, asphalt, tile debris, and structural framing studs.

## Fleet & Rigging Specifications
- **Truck Fleet:** 16-foot high-wall box trucks with roll-up doors, heavy-duty suspension, and gross vehicle ratings capable of carrying up to 4.5 tons (9,000 lbs).
