Specialized Small Structure Demolition Across King & Pierce County

Weather-worn storage sheds, storm-damaged perimeter fences, rotting decks, and neglected outdoor sports enclosures represent serious safety hazards and property eyesores. Instead of renting roll-off dumpsters and risking personal injury with saws and sledgehammers, property owners call M&M Junk Removal for full-service deconstruction:

Our crews arrive fully equipped with commercial reciprocating saws, carbide demo blades, hydraulic lifting gear, and heavy hauling trucks. We systematically deconstruct framing from top to bottom, decouple fasteners, stack materials safely, and haul everything directly to certified Puget Sound recycling centers like DTG Recycle Center in Renton. Once the structure is down, we perform thorough broom sweeps to leave your lawn, patio, or asphalt court completely clean and ready for immediate use.
