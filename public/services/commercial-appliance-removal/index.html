Heavy Commercial Kitchen & Grocery Equipment Handled

Commercial refrigeration units and restaurant ranges are built with dense steel casings, heavy compressors, and hazardous chemical refrigerants. Extracting a 500+ lb commercial reach-in cooler through customer dining spaces, tight hallway turns, or back dock corridors requires specialized mechanical dollies, hydraulic liftgates, and skilled maneuvering.

Our two-technician crews protect commercial floor tiling, doorway molding, and stainless food-prep counters during every extraction. Before final disposal, all cooling systems undergo mandatory EPA Section 608 refrigerant recovery at certified transfer stations.
