# Construction & Remodeling Debris Removal

M&M Junk Removal partners with general contractors, roofers, remodelers, and property flippers across Federal Way, Auburn, Kent, Tacoma, and Puyallup for rapid on-demand debris hauling.

## Debris Handled:
- **Demolition Rubble:** Drywall, sheetrock, plaster, framing lumber, 2x4s, plywood, OSB scraps.
- **Masonry & Heavy Aggregates:** Concrete slabs (chunks up to 400+ lbs), bricks, cinderblocks, pavers, asphalt, ceramic floor tiles.
- **Roofing & Siding:** Cedar shake, asphalt shingles, vinyl siding, flashing, fascia boards.
- **Metal Scraps:** Copper pipes, conduit, iron railings, HVAC ducting, steel studs.

## Fleet Capacity:
- High-capacity 16-foot box trucks with heavy-duty suspension rated up to 4.5 tons (9,000 lbs).
- Commercial hydraulic liftgate for heavy palletized masonry or equipment.
- On-demand live-load sweeps eliminate costly roll-off dumpster street permit fees.

## Transfer Station Logistics:
- Heavy concrete and masonry diverted to certified aggregate recyclers (crushed for road base).
- Clean wood scrap diverted to wood reprocessing yards.
- Solid debris transferred through Bow Lake (Tukwila) or Algona Transfer Station.
