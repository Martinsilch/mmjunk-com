Commercial Vendor & MSA Frequently Asked Questions

How does an M&M Junk Removal Master Service Agreement (MSA) work?

An MSA formalizes a priority partnership between M&M Junk Removal and your organization (general contractor, property manager, or commercial facility). It locks in volume discount pricing for 12 months with a 10% rate beat guarantee below franchise haulers, assigns a dedicated dispatch line, and sets up Net-30 monthly invoicing with zero retainer fees or minimum commitment clauses.

Do you offer Net-30 payment terms for contractors and property managers?

Yes. We provide standard Net-30 commercial terms to verified commercial accounts, licensed general contractors, and active property management firms across Pierce and South King County. Invoices include itemized disposal receipts, scale tickets, and timestamped before-and-after photo documentation.

Can you provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) naming our company as Additional Insured?

Yes. M&M Junk Removal carries $1,000,000+ General Liability commercial coverage, active Washington State L&I workers' compensation, and commercial vehicle policies under WA Active UBI. We issue customized COIs naming your company as Additional Insured within 24 hours of request.

Why choose live-load junk hauling over renting a roll-off dumpster?

Live-load hauling eliminates municipal right-of-way street permit fees ($300-$600+ in Tacoma and South King County), prevents unauthorized neighborhood dumping in your container overnight, keeps commercial parking lots clear, and saves your crew labor since our team handles all loading and hauling.

How quickly can you dispatch a crew for urgent property turnover or eviction cleanouts?

Contract partners receive priority dispatch status with guaranteed same-day or 24-hour response windows throughout South King and Pierce County. For urgent eviction or property restoration cleanouts, we provide dedicated 2-hour emergency response times via our direct owner dispatch line.
