Interior Extraction & On-Site Mechanical Disassembly

Heavy couches, oversized 4-piece sectionals, solid oak armoires, and motorized exercise treadmills are notoriously difficult to maneuver through modern residential doorways. M&M Junk Removal brings professional hand tools to perform on-site disassembly: detaching modular sectional brackets, removing bed frames, and unbolting commercial treadmill uprights so heavy items clear doorways smoothly without scuffs.

Gently used furniture is inspected and prioritized for local Puget Sound charitable donation, while non-reusable mattresses are bundled and delivered to certified regional recycling channels to prevent unauthorized landfill dumping.
