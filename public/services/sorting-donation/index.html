Featured Project: Whole-House Cleanout & 4-Way Sorting in Spanaway, WA

When a family in Spanaway (Pierce County, WA) needed to clean out an entire 3-bedroom residence holding decades of furniture, clothing, records, and household goods, they required more than just heavy lifting. They needed an organized, trustworthy partner to sort through everything systematically before the home could be staged and sold.

How We Executed the Spanaway 4-Stream Triage:

  • 1. Valuables & Records Retention: Examined desks, drawers, and filing cabinets to isolate important deeds, personal financial files, and sentimental heirlooms, placing them safely into family retention bins.
  • 2. Charity Donation Triage: Solid oak china cabinets, 6-drawer dressers, dining chairs, and clean linens were staged and delivered to local Pierce County non-profits (Goodwill and St. Vincent de Paul) with tax receipts provided to the family.
  • 3. Certified Scrap & E-Waste Recycling: Disassembled wooden platform beds, separating steel mounting hardware, wire shelving, and outdated electronics for certified recycling facilities.
  • 4. Regulated Disposal & Broom Sweep: Non-salvageable waste was hauled to Pierce County transfer facilities. The crew performed a full broom-clean sweep of every bedroom, hallway, and garage, leaving the residence completely market-ready.
