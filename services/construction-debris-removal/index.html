Fast Jobsite Turnaround Without Costly Roll-Off Dumpster Permits

Renting roll-off metal dumpsters often requires municipal street permits, damages residential asphalt driveways, and invites illegal neighborhood midnight dumping. M&M Junk Removal offers an on-demand live-load alternative: our crews arrive with high-capacity box trucks, load all renovation scrap immediately, sweep the jobsite, and haul away the debris within hours.

All heavy materials are routed to authorized regional transfer hubs including the Algona King County Transfer Station and Tacoma Recovery & Transfer Center with certified scale tickets. Clean dimensional lumber and scrap metals are segregated for recycling.
