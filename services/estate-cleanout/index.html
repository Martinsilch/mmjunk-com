Compassionate, Systematic Cleanouts for Every Room

Settling an estate or preparing a home for market after decades of accumulation is emotionally exhausting. M&M Junk Removal approaches every estate with respect and discretion. Our team works methodically through master bedrooms, crowded attics, basements, sheds, and garages, identifying personal family keepsakes, documents, and valuables to set aside for the family.

Rather than dumping everything into municipal transfer stations, we actively divert clothing, kitchenware, decor, and gently used furniture to local South Sound non-profits, providing property executors with clean-cleared real estate ready for inspection, appraisal, or open house staging.
