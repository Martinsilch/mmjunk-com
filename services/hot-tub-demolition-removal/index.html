End-to-End Demolition Without Yard Damage

Non-functional hot tubs and deteriorating wooden storage sheds are massive backyard eyesores that harbor pests and consume valuable outdoor living space. Because full hot tubs weigh between 600 and 1,000 lbs empty and cannot squeeze through standard 36-inch yard gates intact, our crews execute precise sectional Sawzall cutting:

First, we verify electrical 240V breaker disconnects and drain any remaining residual water. Next, we slice the acrylic/fiberglass shell into manageable panels, extract the heavy circulation pumps and heater coils for metal recycling, and vacuum/sweep all fiberglass fragments off your deck or concrete patio.
