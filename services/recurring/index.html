Frequently Asked Questions About Recurring & Scheduled Hauling

Does M&M Junk Removal provide municipal weekly curbside garbage collection?

No. We do not provide regular residential weekly curbside trash bin collection or city utility garbage routes (such as Murrey's Disposal in Edgewood or Waste Management in Federal Way). M&M Junk Removal is a private commercial bulk hauler specializing in apartment dumpster corral overflow, bulk tenant furniture removal, and scheduled multi-family cleanouts.

How often can you schedule pickup sweeps for our property?

We offer flexible recurring schedules tailored to your facility: weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or on-demand priority dispatch. Many apartment complexes and HOA communities choose weekly sweeps to keep dumpster corrals clean and avoid city code fines.

Do you require long-term contracts for recurring junk hauling?

No long-term lock-in is required. We offer flexible month-to-month arrangements or 12-month rate locks under a Master Service Agreement (MSA) with Net-14 invoicing and a guaranteed 10% rate beat over big franchise competitors.

Can you set up Net-14 commercial invoicing for our management company?

Yes. We offer Net-14 commercial billing terms for property management firms, HOAs, and corporate accounts. We supply our W-9, Certificate of Insurance ($2M commercial general liability), and automated itemized electronic invoices.

What items can be collected on our scheduled routes?

Our routes handle bulky furniture, abandoned mattresses, appliances (with EPA-certified Freon reclamation), broken shipping pallets, renovation drywall/timber, and yard waste. Hazardous liquids and chemical paints are prohibited.

Can we receive before-and-after photo verification for each scheduled sweep?

Yes. Every route sweep includes digital before-and-after photo manifests and scale tickets delivered via email or commercial portal to ensure complete operational accountability for property managers.
