Driveway Piles to Nutrient-Rich Puget Sound Topsoil

Severe windstorms across South King and Pierce Counties frequently bring down cedar limbs, fir branches, and birch timber. Municipal yard waste roll-carts fill up instantly, leaving property owners with massive brush piles blocking driveways and killing lawn grass.

M&M Junk Removal dispatches crews equipped with heavy pitchforks, chainsaws for timber sizing, and commercial brooms. We load heavy timber cutoffs, shrub clippings, and invasive blackberry vines, sweep the asphalt surface clean, and deliver the load to certified composting facilities where it is ground into industrial organic mulch.
